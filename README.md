## 📌 Problem Statement & Clinical Relevance
India is home to over 101 million individuals diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM). Current standard of care remains predominantly **reactive**—interventions occur only after symptomatic blood glucose surges (>180 mg/dL) or nocturnal hypoglycemic crashes (<70 mg/dL). 

**GlucoTwin** transforms metabolic care from reactive tracking to **proactive simulation**. By synthesizing static Electronic Health Records (EHR) with high-frequency wearable sensor telemetry, GlucoTwin maintains a virtual physiological twin that forecasts glycemic excursions **60 to 120 minutes into the future**.
